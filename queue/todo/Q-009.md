Title: A dead event past the restart limit cannot be replaced inside its window
Kind: Fix
Area: Diagnostics
Size: S
Added: 2026-11-26
---

### Found by 5c-5 (fence 84), outside that row

When the renderer (Caspar) dies more often than the supervisor's restart limit allows (`maxRestarts` within `windowMs`, `process-supervisor.cjs`), the active event stays active with no renderer inside its production window. Stop answers `CONTROL_INHIBITED` (the runtime has no current generation) and a new preparation answers `WINDOW_ACTIVE` (`game-day-manager.cjs` › `policy()`), so the operator cannot replace or end it until the window ends or the app restarts. The diagnostics say `renderer: waiting`, `ndiSender: not-running`, both `SERVICE_FAULT`. Seen in `tests/game-day-recovery.test.cjs`'s renderer test (Linux, synthetic Caspar).

### Success criteria (written 2026-11-26, fence 84, before the work)

Decide and test one bounded way out on Linux: either the operator can end a dead active event (the output stays black, the diagnostics say so) and prepare again, or a later restart is attempted on a bounded schedule. Never replaces a live, acknowledged event; never claims on air. A test fails without the change.
