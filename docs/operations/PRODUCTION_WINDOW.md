# BE-002: NDI sender eligibility policy (software-only)

The `scripts/lib/production-window.cjs` module provides an independently tested production-window policy and serialized start/stop gate. The launcher now applies this eligibility policy to its CasparCG process, including every crash restart. It does not establish an NDI source or prove zero NDI traffic. Real Windows Node process-tree cleanup passed PR #6 CI; CasparCG and off-air network verification remain mandatory before stadium use.

A schedule has explicit ISO 8601 timestamps with `Z` or UTC offsets, e.g. `2026-11-13T19:00:00-06:00`. Ambiguous local timestamps are rejected. The prewarm defaults to 15 minutes, is bounded to 60 minutes, and only makes a sender eligible to start. The end is exclusive. A production window cannot exceed 24 hours. Event metadata must provide the actual offset at each endpoint for DST transitions.

`normalizeWindow(config)` validates the input, `senderAllowed(window, nowMs)` checks eligibility, and `nextBoundary(window, nowMs)` provides the next scheduling boundary. `ProductionWindowGate` serializes idempotent sender start/stop callbacks, retries failed transitions on a later reconcile, and requests stop on shutdown. `LauncherRuntime` uses separate SPX/CasparCG supervisors, schedules production boundaries and polls at most every second (including after wake from sleep). It rechecks eligibility immediately before each launch/restart and after delayed OS spawn. Windows process-tree termination failures are surfaced and retried; no new sender replaces an unconfirmed process. Process-spawn events are not NDI readiness. Process scheduling is not real-time and these checks alone cannot prove packet-level suppression.

Test: `node --test tests/production-window.test.cjs`. The tests cover invalid/ambiguous timestamps, prewarm and end boundaries, DST, duplicate calls, transition failures, and shutdown during startup.

**Hardware gates:** prove no NDI sender/packets outside the window using a local NDI monitor and packet capture, prove sender stability during prewarm and STOP-black, then verify D8VIDEO-P ingest off-air with operator approval. This policy alone is not evidence of NDI suppression.
